03

Common Issues

Lines not drawing, or the ball not bouncing correctly?

  • Make sure you're drawing clearly within the play area
  • Try restarting the app
  • Make sure you're on the latest version from the App Store

High score or coins not saving?

  • Sketch Press saves your progress locally on your device — avoid clearing the app's storage, and make sure your device has free storage space
  • Reinstalling the app resets local progress, since nothing is stored in the cloud

"Watch Ad" button stuck loading or greyed out?

  • Ads need an internet connection — check that you're online
  • A brand-new ad placement can occasionally take a little while to become available; try again shortly
  • Ads may be limited in Airplane Mode or with a very weak connection

App crashing or freezing?

  • Close and fully reopen the app
  • Restart your device if the issue continues
  • Install any available app updates
04

About Rewarded Ads

Sketch Press uses optional, opt-in rewarded ads — for reviving, doubling coins, and earning free coins from the Shop — served by Google AdMob. You'll never see an ad unless you choose to tap "Watch Ad"; nothing plays automatically.

If an ad doesn't load, it's almost always a temporary network or ad-availability issue — not a bug with your progress. Your coins and high score are unaffected either way.
